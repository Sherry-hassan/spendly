Privacy Policy

Last updated · August 27, 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep things simple — you own your data, and we treat it with respect.

◉

1. Data We Collect

To provide Spendly's expense tracking features, we collect a small amount of information from you. This includes the account information you provide when registering (such as your name and email address) and the financial entries you create inside the app — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

We also log basic technical data like your IP address, browser type, and timestamps of activity. This helps us keep the service secure and diagnose issues when they arise. We do not collect any data we do not need to operate Spendly.

◈

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect to operate Spendly, authenticate your account, store your expenses, and present the summaries and insights you see in the app. We also use it to respond to support requests and to keep the service secure and reliable.

We never sell your personal or financial data. We do not use your expense entries to train advertising profiles, and we do not share your information with marketers. Your data is used to provide the service to you — and only for that purpose.

◰

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in secure databases with access restricted to authorized systems. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. We use industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS) and apply reasonable safeguards to protect your information.

While no system is perfectly secure, we work continuously to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. You can request export or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us through the channels listed below.

◊

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly relies on a small set of trusted third-party providers to operate — for example, hosting infrastructure and email delivery for account-related messages. These providers receive only the minimum data needed to perform their service and are bound by confidentiality and security obligations.

We do not embed third-party advertising trackers or analytics that follow you across other websites. Any third-party integrations are listed in this policy and updated as our stack evolves.

✉

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to access or delete your data, or have any privacy-related concerns, please reach out through the support channels in the app. We read every message and respond as quickly as we can.

Thank you for trusting Spendly with your financial data. We take that responsibility seriously.

Questions about your data?

Reach out through the support channels in the app — we're happy to help.
